## What we're building

**Patriot** is our first-responder humanoid robot for fire, disaster and aid. It's meant to go where it's too dangerous to send people first.

Patriot carries swappable **Bunker Packs** on a back mount, so one robot can be fitted for the job at hand:

| Pack | Mission | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **P-01** | Fire suppression | First focus |
| **P-02** | Medical & aid supply | Planned |
| **P-03** | Search & rescue | Planned |

> **Stage:** Patriot is an early concept. We don't have a prototype or field results yet, and we won't claim any until we do.
